OFFICIAL LIBRARY POLICIES & CODE OF CONDUCT

Document Reference: JD-LIB-RULES-2026

01. General Borrowing Quotas

  • Students are permitted to borrow a maximum of three (3) books concurrently.
  • No additional books will be issued if the borrowing count is at capacity.
  • Clearance (No-Dues) is strictly conditional on having zero active loans.

02. Loan Period & Renewal Limits

  • The standard loan period for all textbook copies is fifteen (15) days.
  • A book must be returned or renewed on or before the due date.
  • A single issue transaction can be renewed for a maximum of three (3) consecutive cycles.
  • Upon reaching the 3rd renewal limit, the book must be returned to the library counter before it can be re-issued.

03. Late Returns & Overdue Penalties

  • Any student failing to return or renew a book after the 15-day period will incur a penalty.
  • The overdue penalty is set at a flat rate of ₹10 per day for each overdue day.
  • Fines accumulate automatically in the system and must be paid in full or waived by authorized library personnel before a student can be granted a Clearance Certificate.

04. Care of Library Assets

  • Mutilation, underlining, highlighting, or tearing pages of books is strictly forbidden.
  • In the event of total loss or structural damage, the borrower is liable to replace the volume or pay double the list price.
  • Academic journals and reference periodicals are strictly for in-library reading and cannot be checked out.
